About

Jann is a video editor, filmmaker, creative designer and front-end web developer focused on creating cinematic videos that turn ideas, moments, and stories into meaningful visual experiences. With a strong eye for storytelling, pacing, composition, and sound, he brings together visuals and emotion to create montage films that feel intentional and memorable.

His work goes beyond simply putting clips together. Jann focuses on finding the story within the footage, building the right mood, choosing the right moments, and shaping each scene through editing, music, sound, and visual details. Whether it’s a brand film, event, travel story, or personal project, he aims to create videos that not only look cinematic but also make people feel something.

Jann has worked with a range of clients, with one of his biggest experiences being with the AFL in Australia, where he flew out to film content on-site, capturing real moments and creating visual stories for clients.
